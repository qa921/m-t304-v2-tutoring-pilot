How it works

This pilot explores a simpler, lower-pressure way to ask for tutoring ahead of GCSE or A-level exams. Here's the whole journey:

  1. You tell us the essentials. Year group, subject (and exam board if you know it), and what you'd like to achieve — a target grade or simply feeling more confident.
  2. A real person reads it. Enquiries are reviewed by a human, not auto-matched. If the pilot can't help, we'll say so honestly rather than leave you waiting.
  3. We reply on your chosen channel. Email or phone — your choice, and we only ask for the detail you pick.

What the form asks

Five essential questions: year group, subject and exam board, your goal, and how to reach you. Optional extras — exam date, general availability, lesson frequency and anything else you'd like to add — help us prepare, but you can skip all of them.

What we deliberately don't ask

No full name of the learner is needed at first contact. We never ask for school names, home addresses or medical details. We collect the minimum needed to reply, and nothing more.

What this pilot is — and isn't

Sending the form is an exploratory first contact. It is not a booking, it does not reserve a place, and it does not confirm a tutor match or a lesson. We also don't promise a response time — capacity in a pilot is limited, and we'd rather be upfront about that.
